Privacy

Updated 3 November 2026

WhatsApp Tools is independent desktop software developed by Shivam Patsariya, operating as OmniCan Technologies. It is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Data stored on your computer

The app stores workspace names, tags, preferences, imported contact lists, drafts, message templates, reply rules and self-reported campaign history in your local app profile. Linked WhatsApp sessions keep browser data, including session credentials and cookies, in separate local profiles. Protect access to your computer and its backups.

Broadcast and reply assistance prepare messages for your review. They do not automatically read incoming conversations or send unattended replies. Opening a prepared chat shares its recipient number and message with WhatsApp.

WhatsApp and other third-party services

The embedded WhatsApp Web service communicates directly with WhatsApp. Information you provide there, including your account, contacts and communications, is handled by WhatsApp under its own policies. That processing is separate from the app's local workspace storage. Read WhatsApp's privacy policy and use WhatsApp's account controls for service data requests.

Mac App Store purchases

Apple processes Store purchases. The Store edition uses Apple's StoreKit to verify app ownership locally. The native verification returns the verification result, app bundle identifier and Store environment to the app. It does not send your receipt, Apple account credentials or transaction to an OmniCan licensing server. Direct-download activation keys do not unlock the Store edition.

Direct-download licensing

The separate direct-download licensing service uses live payment verification. Its checkout provider is Razorpay. An order records a hashed installation identifier, provider order/payment identifiers, price, currency and payment status. Requests are rate-limited using a server-secret hash of the requesting IP address and a time bucket. The hosting provider may also maintain access logs. These records support purchase verification, renewal and refunds. Payment details entered into Razorpay's checkout are handled by Razorpay; the app does not store card numbers. Read Razorpay's privacy policy.

Analytics and permissions

The app does not include a developer analytics or advertising SDK. This does not describe or limit the data processing performed by WhatsApp, Apple, Razorpay or the website/hosting providers. Starting with direct-download version 1.0.3, notification, camera and microphone permissions are enabled by default for WhatsApp Web in loaded, activated workspaces. Existing workspaces adopt these defaults once on upgrade. Each can be disabled in account settings; later opt-outs are retained. Camera and microphone access occurs when WhatsApp Web requests it, such as for voice messages, photos or calls. Windows/macOS system approval may also be required. Media you share is processed by WhatsApp under its policies; the workspace app does not independently record it. Other origins and screen sharing remain blocked. Mac App Store availability is separate; the version 1.0.4 Store candidate includes these permission controls.

Your controls

You can remove local workspaces, contacts, drafts and rules from the app. Revoke linked-device access in WhatsApp on your phone. Uninstalling the app may leave its profile or backups on your computer; removing those files permanently removes locally stored app data. Apple and Razorpay provide their own purchase and payment controls.

Support and privacy questions

Contact the developer through OmniCan Technologies. Avoid sending session cookies, activation secrets, private contacts, messages, passwords or payment-card details in support requests.
