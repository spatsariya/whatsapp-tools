WhatsApp reply templates with keyword rules

Use saved replies to prepare consistent answers without rewriting the same information. WhatsApp Tools combines reusable templates, keyword rules and working-hour schedules with a review step before you send.

Prepare a reply in four steps

  1. Create a reusable reply for a common question, such as opening hours or the details you need for an appointment.
  2. Set keyword rules and a working-hour schedule for the draft you want to prepare.
  3. Paste the received message into reply assistance and choose when to generate the draft.
  4. Review the result, make any changes, then open the chat and send the reply yourself.

Use templates for recurring questions

A shop can save a clear opening-hours response. A service business can prepare a list of details needed for a booking. Keep the wording accurate and check dates, prices and availability before sending a saved answer. Templates help with consistency; you remain responsible for the final message.

Schedules guide drafts

Working-hour schedules and keyword rules help prepare an appropriate response to the text you supply. They do not monitor incoming conversations or run an unattended auto-responder. Local rule tests are simulations, and any marked-sent record is self-reported.

Local storage and account choice

Templates and reply rules are stored in your local app profile. Review the prepared text and select the correct account and recipient before sending. Pair reply assistance with separate account workspaces when you handle both personal and work conversations.
