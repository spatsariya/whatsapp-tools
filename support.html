Link an account

Add a workspace and choose a name. On your phone, open WhatsApp → Linked devices → Link a device, then scan the QR code. Each workspace uses a separate linked session. To revoke access, remove the linked device from WhatsApp on your phone.

Messages and replies

Broadcast Studio and reply assistance prepare drafts. You review and press Send in WhatsApp. Marked-sent records are your own activity log and do not confirm delivery.

Mac App Store ownership

The Store edition verifies the app purchase through Apple. Use Check purchase status while signed into the Apple account that acquired the app. A direct-download activation key cannot activate the Store edition. Paid Store distribution is being prepared; do not interpret this page as a claim that the app is already available.

Notifications, camera and microphone

Install direct-download version 1.0.3 or newer. WhatsApp Web receives these app permissions by default. You can turn them off in each workspace’s settings. Reload WhatsApp after changing permissions. If the operating system blocked access, allow WhatsApp Tools in Windows or macOS privacy settings yourself; the app cannot override that decision. Notification delivery also depends on WhatsApp notification settings and system focus modes. Camera/microphone permission does not guarantee that WhatsApp supports calls in your environment. Screen sharing is not enabled.

Get help

Contact OmniCan Technologies or report a non-sensitive problem in the public issue tracker. Include your app version, operating system, the action that failed and a redacted error screenshot. Public issues are visible to everyone: never include phone numbers, contact lists, private messages, session files, credentials or licence keys.
